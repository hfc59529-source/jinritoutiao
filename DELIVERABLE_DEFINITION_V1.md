# Deliverable Definition V2

## Stage 1: Collect

Purpose:
Bring valid source material into the Today Toutiao system.

Deliverable:
老师原文

Next Stage:
Transform

## Stage 2: Transform

Purpose:
Create Today Toutiao-ready content from collected source material.

Deliverable:
头条文案

Output:
头条文案 V1

Next Stage:
Review

## Stage 3: Review

Purpose:
Confirm whether the Toutiao article preserves the source article's distribution potential and meets Today Toutiao publishing standards.

Deliverable:
Review Decision

Next Stage:
PASS: Publish

REVISION: Revision

REJECT: End

## Stage 4: Revision

Purpose:
Revise content only when Review Decision is REVISION.

Deliverable:
最终发布稿

Next Stage:
Review

## Stage 5: Publish

Purpose:
Release approved content to Today Toutiao.

Deliverable:
发布记录

Next Stage:
Feedback

## Stage 6: Feedback

Purpose:
Make post-publish performance visible to the system.

Deliverable:
表现数据

Next Stage:
None
